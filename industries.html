Industries We Serve

We build for sectors where we already know the workflows — which means less time explaining your business and more time solving the problem.

Sectors

Domain knowledge shortens every project

When we already understand your vocabulary, discovery takes days instead of weeks and the first design is much closer to right.

Retail & E-commerce

Online stores that handle real catalogue sizes, real payment failures and real return workflows.

  • WooCommerce and custom storefronts
  • Razorpay, Stripe, PayU and Cashfree integration
  • Inventory sync with Tally or ERP
  • Abandoned cart recovery and offers engine
  • Delivery partner and courier API integration

Manufacturing & Industrial

Shop-floor visibility for plants that are still running on paper registers and WhatsApp photos.

  • Machine uptime and OEE dashboards
  • Sensor-based temperature and vibration monitoring
  • Preventive maintenance scheduling
  • Production and batch traceability
  • Supervisor mobile apps for shift reporting

Logistics & Transport

Tracking, proof of delivery and route planning tools built for drivers who are not sitting at a desk.

  • GPS fleet tracking with geofencing
  • Driver apps with offline-first delivery capture
  • Route optimisation and trip costing
  • Warehouse inward and dispatch modules
  • Customer tracking pages and SMS updates

Education & Training

Portals for schools, colleges and training institutes that parents and students will actually use.

  • Admission and enquiry management
  • Learning portals with video and assessments
  • Attendance, fee and result modules
  • Parent and student mobile apps
  • Certificate generation and verification

Healthcare & Wellness

Clinic and practice software built with privacy and uptime treated as requirements, not extras.

  • Appointment booking and reminders
  • Patient records with role-based access
  • Teleconsultation scheduling
  • Pharmacy and diagnostic order flows
  • Wearable and device data capture

Finance & Insurance

Lead capture, agent tooling and calculators for brokers, NBFCs, advisors and insurance agencies.

  • Agent and partner portals
  • EMI, premium and returns calculators
  • KYC document collection workflows
  • Lead distribution and CRM integration
  • Commission and payout tracking

Professional Services

Credible corporate presence plus the internal tools that keep a services business organised.

  • Corporate websites and microsites
  • Client portals with document sharing
  • Booking and consultation scheduling
  • Proposal and invoice automation
  • Case study and knowledge libraries

Smart Buildings & Energy

Connected infrastructure for societies, commercial complexes and facilities teams.

  • Smart energy metering and billing
  • Access control and visitor management
  • Water tank and pump automation
  • HVAC and lighting scheduling
  • Facility dashboards with alerts
