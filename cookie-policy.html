Cookie Policy

The cookies this site uses, what they do and how to turn them off.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device to remember information about your visit. Similar technologies such as local storage and pixels perform comparable functions. This policy explains which of them https://codeglintsoftwares.com uses and how you can control them.

2. Categories we use

Strictly necessary

Required for the website to function — for example, remembering your cookie preference and protecting forms against abuse. These cannot be switched off through our site.

Analytics

Help us understand which pages are read, how visitors arrive and where they leave. We use Google Analytics 4 for this. The data is aggregated and used to improve the site's content and structure.

Functional

Remember choices you make, such as a form partially completed or a preferred view, so you do not have to re-enter them.

Marketing

Set by advertising platforms where we run campaigns, to measure whether a visit resulted from an advertisement. These are only set if you consent.

3. Third-party cookies

Some cookies are set by services embedded in our pages rather than by us directly. These may include Google Analytics, Google Fonts, Google Maps (on our contact page), and, where used, advertising platforms such as Google Ads and Meta. Each of these providers publishes its own privacy and cookie documentation.

4. How long cookies last

Session cookies are deleted when you close your browser. Persistent cookies remain for a defined period — typically between 24 hours and 24 months depending on their purpose — unless you delete them earlier.

5. Managing cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings: delete existing cookies, block all or specific cookies, or set your browser to warn you before one is stored. Instructions are published by each browser vendor for Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge.

Blocking all cookies will not break this website, though some conveniences will stop working. You can also opt out of Google Analytics across all sites using Google's browser add-on.

6. Do Not Track

Browsers vary in how they implement Do Not Track signals and there is no agreed standard for responding to them. We do not currently alter our behaviour in response to DNT headers.

7. Changes and contact

We update this policy when the technologies on our site change. For any question about cookies, email codeglintsoftware@gmail.com.
