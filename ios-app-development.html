iOS App Development

Swift and SwiftUI apps built to Apple's guidelines, taken from TestFlight beta to a clean App Store approval — without the rejection ping-pong.

Service overview

iOS App Development in short

Apple's review process rejects apps for reasons that have nothing to do with whether the code works — privacy strings, account deletion, payment rules, incomplete metadata. We build to those rules from the start, which is why our submissions usually clear on the first attempt.

We develop native iOS apps in Swift and SwiftUI, sized correctly for iPhone and iPad, and take responsibility for the whole path: design, build, TestFlight distribution to your reviewers, store submission and the update cycle afterwards.

Questions

iOS App Development FAQs

Do I need an Apple Developer account?

Yes — an individual or organisation account costs USD 99 a year, paid directly to Apple. We guide you through enrolment and then publish from your account so you own the listing.

What if Apple rejects the app?

We handle the response and resubmission at no extra cost. Rejections are usually about metadata, privacy declarations or account deletion rules, all of which we address before the first submission.

Can you build iOS and Android together?

Yes. Either natively for both, or in Flutter with one codebase. We will show you the cost difference for your specific feature list before you decide.

How long does App Store review take?

Typically 24 to 48 hours once submitted. Build the review window into your launch date — we plan for a week to be safe.
