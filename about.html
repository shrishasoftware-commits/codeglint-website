About Codeglint Software

A Navi Mumbai engineering studio covering the full stack — websites, mobile apps, custom software and connected IoT hardware — under one roof and one contract.

Our story

A small studio that behaves like an in-house team

Codeglint Software was founded in Navi Mumbai by ICON_Shivani Suryakant Pawar_NAME to solve a problem we kept watching businesses run into: a website built by one vendor, an app by another, and a piece of hardware by a third — with nobody responsible when the three refuse to talk to each other.

So we built a studio that covers the whole line. The same team that designs your interface writes your backend, publishes your mobile app and, when the project calls for it, flashes the firmware on the device sitting in your factory. One contract, one point of contact, one group of people who cannot pass the blame sideways.

We are deliberately small. That means we take on fewer projects than a large agency, and it means the person who answers your call knows exactly what changed in your codebase last Tuesday.

What we believe

Five things we do not compromise on

Write it down first

Every project starts with a document that says what is being built, what is not, and what it costs. Verbal scope is how projects die.

No layers between us

You get the developer's number. Questions get answered by the person who knows the answer, not relayed through three people.

Boring technology wins

We choose frameworks you can hire for in Mumbai next year, not whatever launched last month on Hacker News.

You own everything

Code, designs, domains, hosting, app store listings. All of it registered in your name and handed over on completion.

Honest timelines

We quote what we can deliver. If something slips, you hear it the same week, not on the deadline.

Support is not an upsell

Ninety days of free fixes after launch, because software that breaks in month two was never finished.

Leadership
