Our Process

Discovery to handover in six stages, with a written deliverable and a sign-off at the end of each one. No surprises, no black boxes.

Delivery framework

Six stages, each ending in something you can review

No stage begins until you have signed off on the one before it. That is what keeps projects from drifting.

Discovery & requirement mapping

A structured conversation about the business problem, the people who will use the software, the systems it has to talk to and what "done" looks like. We produce a written requirement document listing every feature in scope — and, just as importantly, everything explicitly out of scope. Deliverable: requirement document, feature list, project charter. Typical duration: 3–7 days.

Proposal, scope & contract

Against that document we prepare a fixed-price proposal broken into milestones, with a delivery calendar and a payment schedule tied to those milestones. You know the total before anything is built. Deliverable: commercial proposal, milestone plan, signed agreement and NDA. Typical duration: 2–3 days.

UI/UX design

Wireframes for every screen first, so we agree on structure before anyone argues about colours. Then high-fidelity design in Figma, with a clickable prototype you can navigate on your own phone. Two rounds of revisions are included at each step. Deliverable: wireframes, visual designs, clickable prototype, design system. Typical duration: 1–3 weeks.

Development sprints

Two-week sprints. At the end of each one there is a working build on a staging server you can open and click through. You get a written sprint summary listing what was completed, what is next and anything that needs a decision from you. Code is pushed daily to a repository in your organisation. Deliverable: staging builds, sprint reports, source code access. Typical duration: 2–12 weeks depending on scope.

Testing & quality assurance

Functional testing against the requirement document, cross-browser checks on Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge, real-device testing on Android and iOS, form and payment validation, load testing where relevant, and a security pass covering the OWASP basics. Every bug is logged, fixed and re-verified before handover. Deliverable: test report, bug log, sign-off checklist. Typical duration: 1–2 weeks.

Launch, handover & support

Deployment to your hosting, DNS and SSL configuration, analytics and Search Console setup, app store submission where applicable, and a recorded walkthrough session for your team. Then 90 days of free bug fixing, with an optional annual maintenance contract after that. Deliverable: live product, credentials, documentation, training session. Typical duration: 3–5 days plus 90 days support.

Communication

How we stay in touch during a build

Silence is the most common complaint people have about development agencies. Here is exactly what you get from us, and when.

  • Daily: WhatsApp or email access to your lead developer for anything urgent.
  • Weekly: a written progress note — what shipped, what is next, what we need from you.
  • Fortnightly: a live demo call on the staging build, 30 minutes, recorded if you cannot attend.
  • Always: a shared task board so you can see the status of every item without asking.
  • On slippage: you hear about a delay the week it happens, with a revised date and the reason.
What we need from you

The three things that decide a timeline

Projects rarely run late because of code. They run late waiting on inputs. If you can cover these, we can hold the date.

  • A single decision-maker. One person with the authority to approve designs and scope. Committees add weeks.
  • Content and assets on time. Copy, logos, product photos, catalogues, certificates — ideally before the design stage ends.
  • Feedback within 48 hours. Consolidated in one message rather than trickling in over a week.
  • Access where needed. Domain registrar, existing hosting, payment gateway or third-party API accounts.
