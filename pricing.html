Pricing

Published starting prices for every service, in USD. No hidden line items, no charge for the proposal, and a fixed price agreed in writing before work begins.

How to read these numbers. Every price below is a real starting point for a real scope, not a teaser. What moves the final figure is the number of screens, how much of the design is custom, and how many external systems we have to connect to. After a short discovery call you get a written fixed price — and it does not change unless you change the scope. All prices are in USD and exclude applicable taxes.

Pricing questions

Before you ask

Can I get a cheaper quote than your starting price?

Below these figures we would have to cut something that matters — testing, revisions or support. What we can do is reduce scope: fewer pages, one platform instead of two, a phased release. Tell us your budget and we will tell you honestly what fits inside it.

Do you charge for the initial consultation?

No. Discovery calls, requirement discussion and the written proposal are free. We only start billing once you sign the scope.

What if the scope changes mid-project?

We quote the change separately with its own price and time impact, and you approve it before we build it. Nothing gets added to your invoice without a written approval from you first.

Are these prices in USD or INR?

All prices on this page are in USD. Indian clients can be invoiced in INR at the prevailing rate with GST applied. UK clients can be invoiced in GBP.
