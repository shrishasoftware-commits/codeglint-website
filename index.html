How we work

A process that removes the guesswork

Six stages, each with a deliverable you can review and sign off before we move on.

1. Discovery

We map what you need, who uses it and what success looks like. Output: a written requirement document.

2. Design

Wireframes first, then visual design. You approve every screen before a line of code is written.

3. Build

Two-week sprints with a live staging link. You test as we go instead of waiting for a big reveal.

4. Testing

Functional, cross-browser, device and load testing. Bugs are fixed before handover, not after.

5. Launch

Deployment, DNS, SSL, analytics, search console and a walkthrough session for your team.

6. Support

90 days of free bug fixing, then an optional AMC for updates, monitoring and enhancements.

Engagement models

Pick the arrangement that fits the work

Fixed price project

Best when the scope is clear. You get a written specification, a milestone schedule and a price that does not move unless you change the scope.

Good for: websites, WordPress builds, MVPs, defined app scopes.

Dedicated developer

A full-time engineer working only on your product, reporting to you daily. Billed monthly, minimum one month, cancel with two weeks' notice.

Good for: evolving products, long roadmaps, in-house team extension.

Hourly / retainer

A block of hours each month for changes, fixes and small features. Unused hours roll over once. Detailed timesheet with every invoice.

Good for: maintenance, ongoing marketing sites, incremental work.

Common questions

Straight answers before you enquire

How much will my project cost?

A brochure website starts at $499, a WordPress business site at $299, a mobile app at $1,499 and an IoT prototype at $2,500. The final figure depends on the number of screens, integrations and how much design is custom. Our pricing page lists every package in detail.

How long does a project take?

Two to four weeks for most websites, four to six for a WordPress e-commerce store, six to twelve for a mobile app, and eight to sixteen for an IoT product that includes hardware. We commit to dates in writing after discovery.

Do I own the code?

Yes. On final payment, all source code, design files, domain and hosting accounts transfer to you. We do not hold anything hostage and we do not use proprietary frameworks you cannot hire for.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Regularly. We work with clients in the UK, US, UAE, Singapore and Australia. Meetings are scheduled in your timezone and invoices are raised in USD or GBP.

What happens after launch?

Ninety days of free bug fixing is included with every project. After that you can take an annual maintenance contract covering hosting, backups, security updates, monitoring and a fixed pool of change hours each month.
