Careers at Codeglint

We hire developers and designers who want to own a whole feature, talk to the client who asked for it, and see it running in the real world.

Why join us

Small team, real ownership

At a large company you would work on one module of one product. Here you will ship a whole feature, talk to the client who asked for it, and see it running in a factory or on someone's phone a month later.

We are honest about the trade-off: fewer layers of support, more responsibility, and you will be expected to make decisions. If that sounds like a problem, we are probably not the right place. If it sounds like the point, get in touch.

Direct client contact

You talk to the people using what you build.

Choose the stack

Engineers weigh in on architecture decisions, not just tickets.

Learning budget

Courses, certifications and conference tickets covered.

Flexible hours

Core hours with hybrid and remote options by role.

Hiring process

Four steps, about two weeks

1. Application

CV plus a link to something you have built. We read everything and reply either way within a week.

2. Intro call

Thirty minutes on your background, what you want to work on and what we do. Ask us anything.

3. Practical task

A small, paid, time-boxed task close to real work. No algorithm puzzles or whiteboard trivia.

4. Final discussion

Walk through your solution with the team, then talk compensation and start date.
